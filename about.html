About us

Interactive tools you can actually use, not PDFs you just read

99DigiProStore is a small Indian digital products store. We make learning, skills, productivity and finance tools that you use, not just read.

Who is behind it

The store is a new initiative by a qualified CMA. It is run as a small sole proprietorship from India. Every product is planned, written and checked by the founder.

What we do

We build ready-to-use digital products in four areas. Each one is a single interactive file that you buy once, download and open in your browser.

  • Learning: practice tools for students and exams, with instant checking.
  • Skills: hands-on courses for software and work skills, where you learn each step and then do it yourself.
  • Productivity: simple tools and guides that save time at work and in everyday life.
  • Finance: calculators and tools for personal and business money matters.

We sell through our Instamojo store, and most products have a free sample on this website. New products are added regularly.

Why we're doing this

Good practice material is often expensive, or it is passive: notes, videos and PDFs you read once and forget. Free material is everywhere, but it rarely tells you what you got wrong or why. We wanted tools that let you practise, see your mistakes straight away and fix them, at a price students and working people can afford.

How we're different

  • Learn by doing. Every exercise has a short lesson before it, then you try it yourself.
  • Instant feedback. Your answers are checked as you go, so you know exactly what to fix.
  • Works offline. Each product is a single file that opens in your browser. No app, no login, no internet needed after download.
  • Made in India. Examples use Indian names, rupees and situations you will recognise.
  • Honest listings. We say clearly what a product covers and what it doesn't. Most products have a free sample, so you can try before you buy.
  • Fair prices. Products start from ₹99. If a file is faulty, we fix it or send a replacement.

What we don't promise

Our products are practice aids. They don't guarantee marks, results or jobs. What they give you is a structured way to practise and to see where you need to improve. The rest is your effort.
