Excel Pro Lab

Advanced Excel practice for people who already know the basics. Each lesson explains the idea with a worked example, then you build the formula yourself on realistic work data and get it checked the moment you press Enter. It covers modern lookups, dynamic arrays, LET and LAMBDA, what-if tools and dashboards, plus a module on using AI to write and check formulas. "Pro" describes the level of the topics. It's one file that works offline.

What you get

  • 13 modules, 66 hands-on exercises and 80 quiz questions
  • Advanced lookups: INDEX/MATCH and XLOOKUP in all its forms
  • Dynamic arrays: FILTER, SORT, SORTBY, UNIQUE, TAKE and SEQUENCE
  • LET and LAMBDA, SUMPRODUCT, MAXIFS and MINIFS, TEXTBEFORE, TEXTAFTER and TEXTJOIN, and finding duplicates
  • PivotTables, conditional formatting, data validation, Goal Seek, Data Tables and Solver
  • 5 chart tasks, a chart quiz and a KPI dashboard
  • Formula auditing, plus Power Query, macros, VBA and Office Scripts as concepts
  • Excel with AI: prompts and hands-on tasks where you check and fix AI formulas. No AI is built in and you need no AI account.
  • An interview quiz and older-Excel alternatives for every modern function
  • 6 capstone projects and a printable certificate of completion
  • A practice spreadsheet and a guide to 116 functions
  • Separate profiles with an optional PIN for shared devices

Who it's for

Working professionals in accounts, finance, sales, operations, HR and MIS, analysts and managers, job seekers preparing for Excel tests, and students who already know the basics. If you're new to Excel, start with Excel - Basics for All.
