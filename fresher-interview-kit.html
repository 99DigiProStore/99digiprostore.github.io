The English round, part by part

Practise out loud. See exactly what to fix.

Tap the mic and speak. Your phone writes down what it heard and marks the words that didn't come through.

1

Read aloud

Read a sentence clearly. Words that weren't clear show in red.

2

Repeat

Hear a sentence once, then say it back. Trains your listening and memory.

3

Short answers

"Which month comes after March?" Answer fast in a word or two.

4

Sentence builds

Put jumbled word groups in order, then say the sentence.

5

Story retell

Hear a short story and retell it in 30 seconds. Tick the key points you covered.

6

Open questions

Speak for 40 seconds with a point, a reason and an example. Compare with a sample answer.
